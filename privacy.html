隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

AKH 3060 rclone 只供擁有者本人使用，只存取擁有者自己的 Google 雲端硬碟中、由擁有者指定的資料夾，用途是同步檔案。

本工具不蒐集、不儲存、不分享任何其他人的資料；授權權杖只保存在擁有者自己的電腦上。擁有者可隨時在 Google 帳戶的「第三方存取權」撤銷授權。

AKH 3060 rclone is used only by its owner. It accesses only folders the owner chooses in the owner's own Google Drive, solely to sync files. It does not collect, store or share anyone else's data; the authorization token is kept only on the owner's computer, and the owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account's third-party access page.
